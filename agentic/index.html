The first spark · Tuesday, March 2

Mountain Hack

See what is possible with open source and agentic AI for the web.

Teams take on real challenges and build agentic solutions for the web on open source and open models, such as Apertus, the open Swiss language model. Coaches accompany every team, and the results go on stage.

Join with a ticket Bring a challenge
  1. 1Frame Pick a challenge and form a team.
  2. 2Build Build an agentic solution for the web on open source.
  3. 3Test Try it with open models and compare what works.
  4. 4Share Present the results to the camp.

Half-day summits

Small rooms, real conversations

Each summit brings practitioners from one sector together to share what works with agentic AI today, and what still holds them back.

  • AI for Education Summit

    For universities, schools and learning platforms: how agents can support teaching and learning on open, sovereign infrastructure.

  • Finance Summit

    For banks, insurers and pension funds: agentic AI under compliance, risk and governance requirements, with open source as the foundation.

Who is it for?

Anyone who builds, runs or decides on AI: developers, product and content teams, and decision-makers from education, finance and the public sector.

No. Agentic Mountain Camp is part of Mountain Camp 2027, so a Mountain Camp ticket gets you in. The Fondue Night is booked separately.
